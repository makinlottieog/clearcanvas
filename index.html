Getting the best result

Pre & post care

Before your appointment

Pre-care

  1. Let hair grow to at least a quarter inch, about the length of a grain of rice, so the wax has enough to grip.
  2. Gently exfoliate the day before to lift dead skin and help prevent ingrown hairs.
  3. Avoid sun exposure or sunbeds for 24–48 hours beforehand.
  4. Skip retinol, exfoliating acids, or other active skincare on the area for a few days prior.
  5. If you're sensitive, avoid caffeine or alcohol shortly before your appointment.
After your appointment

Post-care

  1. Avoid hot showers, baths, saunas, and swimming pools for 24 hours.
  2. Stay out of the sun or sunbeds for 24–48 hours — freshly waxed skin is more sensitive to UV.
  3. Wear loose, breathable clothing to avoid irritating the area.
  4. Don't touch, pick, or exfoliate the area for 24–48 hours.
  5. Once healed, exfoliate regularly to help prevent ingrown hairs.
  6. Keep the area moisturised daily, starting the following day.
  7. Hold off on deodorant, perfume, or scented products on the area for 24 hours.
  8. Avoid heavy sweating or intense workouts for 24 hours to reduce irritation.
  9. Steer clear of chlorinated pools and hot tubs for 48 hours.
  10. Some redness or small bumps are normal and usually settle within a day.
  11. Book your next appointment for 4–6 weeks' time to keep on top of regrowth.
  12. If irritation persists beyond a couple of days, get in touch and I'll advise.
